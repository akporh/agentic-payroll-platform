## Headline readiness summary

- **Ready or ready-with-normal-work**: C6, C14 (2 of 15)
- **Conditionally ready**: C5, C11 (2 of 15)
- **Blocked**: C1, C2, C3, C4, C7, C8, C12, C13 (8 of 15) — notably, this is a majority of the portfolio, and includes two capabilities (C3, C7) that Stage 04 did not previously flag as blocked but this stage's re-verification confirms are gated on foundational infrastructure (C1/C2) or, for C7, on the exception-resolution workflow per D-04-01's own binding condition.
- **Deferred**: C10, C15 (2 of 15)
- **Rejected**: C9 (1 of 15)

**The single most consequential fact this stage adds**: C1 (auth) has zero implementation today, and nearly every "blocked" capability above is blocked at least partly because of this one shared, foundational gap. This was previously understood as a sequencing preference (Stage 04's "C1 → C2 → ..." signal); this stage's evidence makes it a hard, currently-unmet precondition for the majority of the portfolio.
