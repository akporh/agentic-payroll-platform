# Stage 04 → Stage 11 Handoff (Commercial & Product Strategy)

## Sequencing signal from `outcome-prioritisation.md`

Not a roadmap, but an evidence-based sequencing signal: **C1 → C2 → (C6, C12, C14 in parallel, all deterministic) → exception-resolution-workflow design → C3/C5/C13/C11 (each individually gated) → C4/C8 only once Stage 05 closes their blockers.** Offered for Stage 11 to weigh against commercial priorities, not a committed plan.

## Outcomes that are squarely Stage 11's remit, not Stage 04's

- **Client profitability/operational-cost insight** (`product-opportunity-map.md` area 15) — classified "defer" in this stage's prioritisation, but explicitly because it's a business-strategy question, not because it lacks value. Stage 11 should assess this against actual commercial goals.
- **Operational reporting and continuous improvement** as a whole (F-04-06) — a genuinely missing lifecycle area with no current capability; Stage 11 should decide whether/when this becomes a commercial initiative, once its platform prerequisite (audit-coverage fix) is scoped by Stage 05.

## Baseline-instrumentation recommendations with commercial relevance

- Onboarding baseline metrics (F-04-03) — time-to-go-live and parallel-run agreement rate are commercially relevant (faster, more confident client onboarding is a sales/delivery differentiator), not purely internal engineering metrics. Stage 11 may want to weigh in on whether instrumenting these ahead of C13/C14 is worth prioritizing for its own sake.
- Compliance chain timing (`compliance-outcome-chain.md`) — time-to-detection/time-to-apply for statutory changes is a genuine commercial differentiator for a payroll bureau (faster compliance response than manual competitors) — worth Stage 11 considering as a positioning point, independent of the technical build sequence.

## What Stage 11 should NOT re-derive

The technical dispositions of the 15 approved capabilities (already fixed by Stage 03's gate) or the platform/compliance prerequisites (Stage 05/06's remit) — Stage 11's job is commercial framing and prioritisation against business strategy, using this stage's outcome/evidence work as input.
