# Stage 12 Output: Product-Direction Narrative (Q3)

The coherent story Stage 13's roadmap tells over time — synthesised from Stage 11's positioning inputs (`positioning-and-claims.md`, `commercial-value-map.md`), the Stage 10 assurance posture, and the retained thesis. This is the substance of the source document's revision (see `source-document-disposition.md`); it is **not marketing copy**, and no claim below may be used externally before its backing artifact exists and is green. Competitor characterisations are conditional/definitional only — no unregistered competitive-landscape claims (Stage 11 critic note, carried).

## 1. The organizing frame

**"A payroll platform with an assistant."** The deterministic engine is the product's core; AI is a bounded assistance layer whose every action is logged, attributed, and evidence-linked. Demos and narrative lead with the **exception queue, approval screens, and audit surfaces** — where auditable AI is visible — and treat chat as one capability's surface (Principle 8; Stage 09 surface reality; `positioning-and-claims.md` §1).

## 2. The three stories, in the order they become tellable

The commercial value order *matches* the technical readiness order (DEC-11-03) — the narrative unfolds in the same sequence the platform is built, with no build-vs-value tension:

**Story 1 — Compliance you can inspect (C12, then C11).** Statutory changes apply only through recorded, re-authenticated human approval (claimable deterministically once C12 ships — CG-12/SG-12 evidence set); monitored detection follows (C11), bounded exactly by the Tier-1 source allowlist and cadence, measured as time-to-detection/time-to-apply against the B5 baseline (retrospectively capturable now from NTA 2025 → PAY-TAX-1). Success is precision, never detection volume (D-04-01).

**Story 2 — Onboarding with evidence (C14, then C13).** Dry-run-evidenced client onboarding: deterministic import validation and a real-path dry run (C14) carry claimable value before any AI mapping exists; the AI mapping assistant (C13) follows, its improvement claims anchored to the B1/B2/B3 baselines — which must be captured on a real onboarding *before* C13 ships (unrecoverable window W2, EG-004). Dry-run-pass and client-validated accuracy remain two metrics, never collapsed.

**Story 3 — Input quality that earned its numbers (C7).** Calibrated anomaly detection with published false-positive/false-negative discipline — the platform's clearest "AI that earns trust" exhibit, on a structurally lagged timeline (shadow exit ≥ 3 cycles + ≥ 20 terminal records, W1). Month-one C7 value claims are wrong by construction; the lag *is* the story.

**The cross-capability differentiator running under all three**: the assurance posture itself — continuously verified tenant isolation (SS-1), evidence-linked AI actions (SC-3), human-approved statutory change (CG-12), measured calibration — four standing-artifact-backed properties of the *platform*, checkable by a client's auditor (`positioning-and-claims.md` §2).

## 3. The supporting cast, and what is deliberately not led with

- C3 (assistant), C5 (trace narration), C6 (readiness service) carry genuine operator/client value but are **wrong as commercial leads** (`commercial-value-map.md` §2) — they make the operator faster; they don't differentiate the bureau.
- **Non-value rows are binding**: C4/C8 (blocked), C9 (rejected), C15 (deferred) contribute nothing to the narrative until their own conditions change — no timeline, demo, or claim may assume they unblock (O7; overclaim table).
- C6's readiness panel is never presented as an exhaustive pre-flight check (it covers three named conditions — measurement-framework harmful-incentive note).

## 4. The honesty asymmetry — the position itself

The overclaim table (`positioning-and-claims.md` §3) is a **hard boundary and the position**: never "tamper-proof," never unbounded "you'll never miss a statutory change," never usage-volume or detection-volume success stories, never dry-run-equals-accuracy, never value ahead of gate evidence. Competitors — defined here conditionally as manual bureaus or AI products without assurance discipline — can say "AI-powered" louder; the platform's counter is **"here is the standing test suite, the audit chain, and the calibration report — check them."** Honest boundaries (in-DB tamper-evidence, bounded monitoring guarantees, measured-not-asserted improvements) read as credibility to exactly the audience a payroll bureau serves. The *pace* of claims — post-gate, post-baseline — is a feature of the trust story, not a delay to apologise for (§4 of `positioning-and-claims.md`).

## 5. What the narrative waits on

- **Capability-led until demand evidence exists**: every claim above is *provable*, none is *proven to sell* (F-11-02, EG-005). External-facing use of any story waits on Michael's client-side facts.
- **Baseline-before-build windows** thread the narrative's credibility: B5/B3 retrospectives capturable now (W5); B1/B2 hinge on the next real onboarding (EG-004); B6/B4 tallies pinned to C3/C6 sprint planning (W3/W4).
- **The fork stays framed**: the narrative is a single-bureau story on a SaaS-ready substrate; an active SaaS narrative requires the human decision bundle first (`target-direction-statement.md` §5).
