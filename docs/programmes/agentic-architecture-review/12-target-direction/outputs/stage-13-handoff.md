This handoff is consistent with `11-commercial-product-strategy/outputs/sequencing-economics.md` and `pre-build-decision-logistics.md` — it adds the direction layer on top of them and duplicates neither.

## 1. The direction, in the form Stage 13 consumes

- **Direction statement** (`target-direction-statement.md`): a payroll platform with an assistant — deterministic core, five gated AI capabilities, differentiation visible in queue/approval/evidence surfaces; single-operator, capped-cadence bureau reality; **default direction: single-bureau excellence with SaaS-ready posture** (the fork's alternative is a human bundle, §5).
- **Posture constraints** (`target-architecture-posture.md` P-A…P-H): every roadmap item is checkable against them; violating one requires a recorded human decision (ratchet).
- **End-state map** (`capability-end-state-map.md`): what "arrived" looks like per capability with its closure evidence — the roadmap's per-item definition of done rides the launch-gate register's "done = row green" rule.
- **Narrative** (`product-direction-narrative.md`): the story the roadmap tells time over — three stories in readiness order (compliance → onboarding → input quality) on the assurance-posture differentiator; claims paced by gates and baselines.
- **Direction KPIs** (`direction-kpis.md` K1–K6): the review-back measures; quarterly session slot, no new standing obligation.

## 2. Sequencing input (inherited whole, not restated)

The full constraint set is Stage 11's: orderings O1–O9, windows W1–W6, one-off cost placements, the capped cadence, and the value-priority signal **C1 → C2(+exception substrate) → C12 & C14 → C6/C3/C5 → C7 shadow → C11 → C13** (Stage 11 → 13 handoff §4).
